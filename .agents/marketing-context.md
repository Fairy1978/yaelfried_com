## 1. Product Overview

**One line** 🟢
Trance and therapeutic suggestion, working directly with the subconscious to release the patterns that keep repeating.

**What it is** 🟢
A one-to-one guided process. Sessions run 60 to 90 minutes, five initial sessions, more if needed. In each session the client enters trance, connects to the subconscious, and releases another layer. Most women see significant change within 5 to 8 sessions.

**Category (the shelf people search on)** 🟡
Hebrew: טראנס וסוגסטיה טיפולית, היפנותרפיה, עבודה עם תת המודע.
This sits next to hypnotherapy in how people search, though the site does not lead with the word היפנוזה.

**Practitioner title** 🟢 מנחה תודעתית. **Not** a licensed psychologist or psychotherapist. Claims must stay away from medical or clinical treatment language.

**Business model** 🟡 Paid one-to-one sessions. First "process session" offered at a special price as the entry point.

**Brand name** 🟢 הדרך חזרה אלייך / The Way Back to Yourself (adopted 2026-07-27, replacing המסע אל עצמך).

## 2. Target Audience

**Who** 🟢
Women who have already tried everything: therapy, workshops, books, podcasts. It helped a little but did not change daily reality.

**Note the contradiction** 🔴
The Hebrew homepage carries the line "הליווי שלי מתאים לנשים וגברים כאחד", but essentially all copy is written in feminine second person and the treatment page opens "אני מלווה נשים שחוות". **This needs a decision**, because it changes keyword targeting: either the audience is women and the line should go, or men are genuinely welcome and the copy needs to stop excluding them.

**The core problem** 🟢
The same reaction that repeats even after promising yourself it would be different this time. The same conversation with the same person that always ends the same way. Thoughts going round the same circle. Acting against yourself and not knowing why. Standing still while life carries on.

**What they are hired to do** 🟡
1. Stop the loop at the root rather than manage it
2. Get relief fast, in a defined number of sessions, not open-ended therapy
3. Have change that holds instead of fading

## 4. Differentiation

**The mechanism, and it is the whole argument** 🟢
The conscious mind is 5% of the brain. The subconscious is 95%. Everything the client already tried spoke only to the 5%. Patterns live in the 95%, where willpower does not reach. Trance opens access; suggestion is the language that speaks there. Change made there is permanent.

**Credibility** 🟢
PhD in biology. 20 years at the Weizmann Institute, then foodtech and biotech. 15 years of parallel inner exploration. The site explicitly grounds trance in neurology: alpha and theta waves, prefrontal cortex quieting, limbic system opening, parasympathetic activation.

This science-plus-subconscious combination is the real differentiator. Very few practitioners in this space can carry it honestly.

**Speed and boundedness** 🟢 Under 10 sessions. Named, finite, unlike open-ended therapy.

## 6. Brand Voice

**Tone** 🟡 Warm, direct, calm. Speaks to the reader as "את". Never hype, never guru. Names the exhaustion plainly before offering anything.

**Personality** 🟡 Precise, grounded, human, unhurried, quietly confident.

**Do** 🟡 Short declarative sentences. Name the specific daily experience rather than the abstraction. Explain the mechanism. Let the science carry the authority.

**Avoid** 🟡 Promises of cure. Clinical or medical claims. Pressure. Mystical framing.

**Open question on mechanics** 🔴
Yael's stated writing preferences include no em dashes and no negative or doubtful framing. But the breaktheloop title currently reads "יעל פריד — שברי את הלופ" with an em dash. **Needs a ruling**: does the no-em-dash rule apply to site copy, or only to how Claude writes to her?

**Structural findings, verified on the live pages 2026-07-27**
- All 6 Hebrew pages share one title and one description. Same for the 6 English pages.
- Zero headings of any level on any page. Framer exported everything as plain boxes.
- No lang attribute; Hebrew pages are marked dir="ltr".
- No robots.txt, no sitemap.xml on yaelfried.com.
- Descriptions overrun: Hebrew 202 characters, English 281, against roughly 155 shown.
- hreflang between Hebrew and English pairs **is** correctly configured. Leave it alone.
- Google Search Console is verified on the domain (verification meta tag present).
- breaktheloop and start compete with yaelfried.com for the same Hebrew terms. Both are ad destinations. Open decision on whether to hide them from Google.
