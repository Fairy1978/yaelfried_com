# SEO Audit — all four Yael Fried properties

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Scope:** Technical + on-page, all four sites
**Evidence:** live pages fetched and inspected in a browser, plus source files. Every finding below was verified, not assumed.
**Not available:** keyword volume data. Search Console is verified on the domain but not yet shared, so target terms are reasoned from site copy, not measured.

---

## Executive Summary

1. **All 12 pages of yaelfried.com carry one title and one description.** Six Hebrew pages identical, six English pages identical. The site cannot rank for anything specific because every page tells Google the same thing.
2. **breaktheloop's title uses an imperative verb (שברי) that nobody types into a search box.** This is the strongest page in the funnel and its title is unsearchable.
3. **yaelfried.com has no sitemap and no robots.txt.** Google is crawling it blind.
4. **Not one heading element exists on any page of the main site.** Framer exported everything as plain boxes. Google has no structural signal and screen readers have nothing to navigate.
5. **start.yaelfried.com is live, indexable, and argues the old trauma/anxiety positioning** that the rebrand replaced.

**Quick wins:** breaktheloop title verb, em dash swap, robots.txt + sitemap, thanks-page hidden. All small, all same-day.

## Technical Findings

### T1. No sitemap.xml or robots.txt on yaelfried.com
- **Impact:** High
- **Evidence:** both return 404 on the live domain. breaktheloop has both.
- **Fix:** publish a sitemap listing all 12 pages with their clean URLs, plus a robots.txt pointing to it.
- **Priority:** 1

### T2. Hebrew pages do not declare their language, and are marked left to right
- **Impact:** High
- **Evidence:** `<html>` on yaelfried.com has no `lang` attribute and carries `dir="ltr"` on Hebrew pages. Checked live on /about-he.
- **Fix:** `lang="he" dir="rtl"` on the six Hebrew pages, `lang="en" dir="ltr"` on the six English ones.
- **Priority:** 1
- **Caution:** flipping `dir` on a Framer export can move the layout. Must be checked visually on every page before publishing.

### T3. Zero heading elements on any page of the main site
- **Impact:** High
- **Evidence:** `document.querySelectorAll('h1').length` returns 0 on the live pages. So does h2.
- **Fix:** promote the existing visual headlines to real heading tags. One h1 per page, h2 for section titles.
- **Priority:** 2
- **Note:** this is simultaneously the site's biggest accessibility gap. One fix serves both. Hand to `a11y-audit` to do it once, properly.

### T4. hreflang is correctly configured
- **Impact:** positive, no action
- **Evidence:** each page links its Hebrew and English twin plus x-default. Verified live.
- **Fix:** none. Do not touch.

### T5. links.yaelfried.com has no canonical
- **Impact:** Low
- **Priority:** 4

### T6. start.yaelfried.com is fully indexable and contradicts current positioning
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Evidence:** no robots directives, no canonical, three pages sharing one title, thank-you page indexable.
- **Fix:** retire the domain with a permanent redirect. GitHub Pages cannot issue one, so move the domain to Vercel where it is one config line.
- **Priority:** 3

## On-Page Findings

### O1. Twelve pages, two titles, two descriptions
- **Impact:** Critical
- **Evidence:**
  - 6 Hebrew pages all read `יעל פריד | לצאת ממעגל התקיעות | טראנס וסוגסטיה`
  - 6 English pages all read `Yael Fried | Break Free from the Cycle | Trance & Suggestion`
  - descriptions likewise identical within each language
- **Fix:** one title and one description per page, each targeting a distinct cluster, each carrying the stuckness/loop frame.
- **Priority:** 1

### O2. breaktheloop title uses an imperative nobody searches
- **Impact:** Critical for the funnel's best page
- **Evidence:** title is `יעל פריד — שברי את הלופ`. שברי is a command form. Search queries use infinitives and nouns.
- **Fix:** `לשבור את הלופ | לצאת מתקיעות | יעל פריד`
- **Priority:** 1

### O3. Em dashes in breaktheloop's title and description
- **Impact:** Low for ranking, real for brand consistency
- **Evidence:** present in `<title>`, `meta description` and `og:title`, all of which display to users. Clean everywhere else across all four sites.
- **Fix:** replace with `|`, matching the wordmark.
- **Priority:** 2

### O4. Descriptions overrun what Google displays
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Evidence:** Hebrew 202 characters, English 281, against roughly 155 displayed. Both truncate mid sentence.
- **Fix:** rewrite to about 150.
- **Priority:** 2

### O5. start's thank-you page is indexable
- **Impact:** Medium while the site is live
- **Fix:** resolved by the retirement in T6.
- **Priority:** 3

## Content Findings

### C1. The condition list is the site's largest untapped asset
- **Evidence:** treatment-he.html covers repeating patterns, stuckness, creative blocks, procrastination, relationships, low self-image, perfectionism, anxiety, panic, phobias (heights, flying, enclosed spaces), social anxiety, fear of failure and rejection, insomnia, nightmares, low mood, trauma, childhood trauma, PTSD, chronic pain, fibromyalgia, chronic headaches, smoking cessation.
- **Problem:** all of it sits on one page whose title mentions none of it.
- **Fix:** each cluster earns its own targeting, framed as a loop rather than as clinical treatment, consistent with Yael's stated preference for people who have tried everything over people seeking medication.
- **Priority:** 2

### C2. The strongest existing copy should be reused, not rewritten
- "את עייפה מלמצוא את עצמך שוב באותו מקום."
- "את עייפה מלקוות שמשהו סוף סוף ישתנה."
- "את עייפה מלחיות ככה."
- These are the site's best lines and read as search-adjacent language for someone stuck.

### C3. Audience contradiction
- Homepage states the work suits women and men. Treatment page opens "אני מלווה נשים שחוות" and all copy is feminine second person.
- Yael has confirmed the audience is both.
- **Consequence for SEO:** copy and metadata should stop being exclusively feminine, or the site will keep filtering out half the intended audience.
- **Priority:** 2, and it is a copy job rather than a metadata job.
