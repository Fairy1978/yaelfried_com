## Reel 5, smoking. The highest exposure in the set
- **5.1 (H) A cessation claim.** `הסיגריה מאבדת את התפקיד שלה` flatly states that after her process the
  cigarette stops doing its job. With a question that literally asks about quitting, the reel reads as
  a smoking cessation service with a stated result. Cessation claims are the most scrutinised claims in
  this field, nicotine dependence is a physical condition, and she already has a public testimonial
  about someone stopping after one session, which makes an implied guarantee easy to pin on her.
  `וכשהעצירה הופכת לזמינה מעצמה, הסיגריה מאבדת את התפקיד שלה.` →
  `וכשהעצירה הופכת לזמינה מעצמה, הסיגריה יכולה לאבד את התפקיד שלה. הבחירה עצמה נשארת אצלך.`
  The second sentence is the compliance sentence. Keeping the decision with her is what separates
  guidance from a service acting on a dependency. Reel 5 has the room, 78 to 83.
- **5.2 (L) Keep the question.** I tested moving the reel off quitting and onto the four minutes and
  recommend against. The answer never tells her she will stop. The risk sat only in the payoff verb.
- **5.3 (L)** `וזאת ההפסקה היחידה שמגיעה עם אישור` is an absolute. Low risk, it earns its place.

## Reel 11, does it hold. The question I was asked to rule on
**Ruling: as written it is a promise, and the clearest one in the set.** The question asks whether the
change lasts. The answer says `לכן היא מחזיקה מעצמה`. An unqualified yes, about a future she cannot
control, on the one axis where a disappointed client later says she was told it would last.
The positioning underneath is defensible. Behaviour needs daily willpower, meaning sits where it was
created, so there is a maintenance difference, and that is a mechanism argument she may make. The
promise lives only in the verb.
- **11.1 (H)** `לכן היא מחזיקה מעצמה, והיום שלך משוחרר מתחזוקה.` →
  `לכן היא נוטה להחזיק מעצמה, והיום שלך משוחרר מתחזוקה.`
  One word, and it stops being a guarantee and becomes her account of how it behaves.
- **11.2 (M) Survivorship read as a rule.** Only the women who came back are counted, and the sentence
  covers all of them. `נשים שחוזרות אליי שנה אחרי מספרות` →
  `יש נשים שחוזרות אליי שנה אחרי ומספרות`
- **11.3 (L)** `עברת כבר תהליכים שהחזיקו חודש` is on brand. She has tried a lot. Keep.
