# Seat 2, the Hebrew direct response copywriter, 2026-09-20

Every replacement was run against the negation list and the full Session 1 plus 16 reel trigram
corpus. Zero negation words, zero new three word collisions. Word deltas marked so the band holds.
Reels at the 78 floor are marked FLOOR.

**The pattern under all of it.** Spoken Hebrew uses verbs, written Hebrew uses nouns made out of
verbs. Wherever a reel limps aloud a nominalization is sitting in it: החזקה, עשייה, הכניסה, הנמכת
עוצמה, תחזוקה, הבחירה נסגרת, הסריקה. Nine of fifteen have one. Noun back to verb fixes most of it.

## Reel 2
1. `וכעבור חודשיים` is written. → `ואחרי חודשיים` (0)
2. **`אקלים רגשי` is the stiffest phrase in the batch**, a literal translation of emotional climate, twice. No Israeli woman says it aloud.
   `הוא מזהה אקלים רגשי שגדלת בתוכו, ומסמן אותו כבטוח.` → `הוא מזהה את האווירה שגדלת בתוכה, ומסמן אותה כבטוחה.` (0)
   `...הרגע שבו האקלים קיבל את השם בית.` → `...הרגע שבו האווירה קיבלה את השם בית.` (0)
3. `לכן הבחירה נסגרת כבר בפגישה הראשונה` is a passive nominalization in a 14 word sentence that collapses aloud. → `לכן את כבר בוחרת בפגישה הראשונה, בשנייה שמשהו בך נרגע דווקא מולו.` (−2)
4. `אדם רגוע` is the written word. In speech about dating it is `מישהו`. → `מישהו רגוע מתחיל להיות מעניין.` (0)
5. `ומנסחת הודעה בראש` is abstract. → `ומוחקת הודעה פעמיים לפני ששולחת.` (0)
Net −2, lands at 83.

## The hooks, ranked
Strong: 7, 13, 5, 8, 6. Every one opens with a body doing something in a room.
Weak: 3, 10, 12, 15, and 9 behind them. Every one opens with a mental verb, `מחשבת`, `מדמיינת`,
`קוראת`, `מחכה`, `מניחה`. Nothing happens at second one, so nothing stops the thumb. The rule to
enforce on any rewrite: **the first four words are a body, never a thought.** Reels 3 and 10 must move.

## The closers
Reels 8, 12, 14 and 15 end without asking for anything, and 8, 13 and 14 close on the same
reassurance, you show up and I lead. Back to back it stops sounding generous and starts sounding like
a script. Against that, four reels (2, 3, 10, 16) all ask for a name and a number. Spread them: keep
name and number in 10 and 16 only, a question in 2 and 9, a plain `בואי נדבר` in 8, one line in 12 and 15.

## The change I would fight hardest for
**Kill `אקלים רגשי` in reel 2, both instances.** Everything else here is a reel sounding slightly
written. That one is a reel sounding translated. It is the mechanism sentence, the line the whole reel
turns on, and the one phrase in fifteen scripts that a woman watching will hear and think, someone
wrote this for her. `האווירה שגדלת בתוכה` costs zero words and says the same thing in her voice.
