# Seat 1, the ideal client. 43, done therapy twice, scrolling at 23:40.

Judged on three things. Does the opening make me feel seen in a specific physical way, is the
answer something I have never heard, and at the end do I want to write to her or do I feel
handled. Every replacement is a swap in place, re-run `tests_s2.py`.

## Reel 2, the same kind of relationship
The mechanism is the most-heard claim in this niche. The subconscious chooses the familiar, the
familiar feels like home, it is the climate you grew up in. I have heard that in every podcast
since 2015, and it blames my parents in one sentence from a stranger. The only new thing is
buried at line 8: the choice closes at the first meeting, in the second something in me relaxed.
Lead with it, cut the climate lecture. Line 10, a calm person starts being interesting, is the
best line in the batch and the thing nobody says out loud. Make it the payoff.
Replace lines 6 to 8 with:
> את מרגישה קליק, וזאת בעצם רגיעה. הגוף שלך זיהה משהו שהוא כבר יודע לתפעל.
> הרגיעה הזאת היא הסימן, והיא מגיעה מוקדם, הרבה לפני שהכרת אותו.

## Reel 4, the head starts racing in bed

most-heard explanation on the internet: by day you are busy, at night it is quiet so the thoughts
come. That is a restatement of my question. Line 34, night as permission rather than malfunction,
is the only thing worth keeping, and it arrives as an assertion.
Replace lines 29 to 30 with:
> את מכבה את האור ומסדרת את הכרית פעמיים. ואז הראש מתחיל את הסיבוב.
Replace lines 31 to 33 with:
> במשך היום את מחזיקה, ובלילה ההחזקה מתפנה לרגע. המערכת שלך מנצלת בדיוק את החלון הזה.

## Reel 7, I recognize the pattern and it still happens
The percentages are where I decide she is the same as everyone else. Five and ninety five has
been in every hypnosis ad for a decade, it is the most challengeable claim in the batch, and it
turns her from a person into a practitioner. The real answer is already at line 69: my knowing is
an accurate commentator arriving one second late. New, humbling rather than flattering, enough.
Replace lines 67 to 69 with:
> הידיעה שלך מדויקת, והיא מגיעה שנייה אחרי. התגובה כבר יצאה, והיא מסבירה אותה יפה.
> החלק שמגיב ראשון פועל ממקום אחר לגמרי, והוא כבר החליט.
The closing, a conversation about that second, is the best matched invitation in the batch.

## Reel 11, will it hold
Line 113, you told yourself that maybe it is simply you, is the truest sentence in all fifteen
reels. Leave it untouched. The real addition is maintenance: a change I hold by willpower costs
me daily, this one asks nothing of me. Make that the spine and the reel stops restating reel 1.
Line 119 is the claim I would screenshot for a friend with an eye roll. A year later they say it
just stayed is unfalsifiable, and it is what anyone selling anything says.
Replace line 119 with:
> נשים כותבות לי שנה אחרי, בדרך כלל על משהו קטן שהן עשו בקלות.

## Reel 12, what if I am disappointed again
The opening is me, literally, watching this reel, and line 11, putting it on the table if her way
is far from what I need, is the most trust-building sentence in the batch. But there is no second
beat. No mechanism, no insight, nothing I did not know. The offer is a free conversation, which
every practitioner on my feed offers, so it reads as an ad. It also answers the smaller fear.
Mine is that I will find out the problem is me and that everything slides off me. Nobody in
fifteen reels says that. Insert after line 7:
> מתחת לזה יש שאלה גדולה יותר. אם גם זה יעבור לידך, מה זה אומר עלייך.
> השמירה הזאת היא סימן טוב. המערכת שלך למדה מהר, והיא תזהה גם את מה שכן מתאים לך.
